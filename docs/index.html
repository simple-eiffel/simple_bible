Overview

simple_bible is a free Bible-study workbench for Windows. An Eiffel engine over SQLite looks up, counts and compares the biblical text in Hebrew, Greek and English, and every fact it shows carries its source and license.

It is written for the reader who has outgrown a free reader but does not want to buy into an expensive library, and for anyone who wants to check a claim about the text (including an AI's claim) against the text itself.

Status

Design and contracts. Not usable yet. Built with the Eiffel Spec Kit:

  • Done: research, GUI layout spec, specification, approved intent
  • Done: Phase 1 engine contracts — 150 BIB_* classes, full contracts and MML model queries, 0 errors and 0 warnings
  • Next: design-fork debates, then Phase 2 contract review as a separate-seat debate cycle

Principles

  • The engine owns every fact. A fact cannot exist without a source, and every result cites the sources it used. This is enforced by contract.
  • Counts show their failures. Every pattern or count result carries FITS, PARTIAL, FAILS and NO_DATA, and can run beside frequency-matched control words.
  • AI is optional and never a source of facts. Release 1 runs no AI on your machine.
  • No ads, store, accounts, telemetry, activation or paywalls. Your notes stay yours.
  • Ordinary hardware. CPU-only, 8 GB of RAM, no GPU needed.

Design

The interface is native simple_widgets with shaped right-to-left Hebrew. It opens in a Simple mode (a three-pane reader where every pane follows the verse) and offers a Study mode (docked panels, linked sets, search, guides, counts and the author library). The full research, specification and GUI spec live in .eiffel-workflow/ in the repository.

Building

cd simple_bible
/d/prod/ec.sh check -config simple_bible.ecf -target simple_bible_tests
/d/prod/ec.sh test  -config simple_bible.ecf -target simple_bible_tests

Dependencies: base, simple_mml, simple_sql, simple_datetime; tests add testing and simple_testing.
